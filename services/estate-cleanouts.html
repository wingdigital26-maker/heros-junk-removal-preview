A whole home, handled with care, on your timeline

Whether you are settling a loved one's estate, clearing a home for sale, or handling a big downsizing move, Hero’s clears whole houses across DFW in one to two days. We start with a walkthrough, agree on exactly what stays and what goes, and give you one firm price and timeline. Then the crew works room by room while you handle everything else on your plate. Families, executors, realtors, and property managers all use the same process. Our estate cleanout checklist lays out how most families sort a house before the crew ever pulls up, and where to donate furniture in DFW covers what happens to the pieces that still have life in them.

The keepsake protocol

This is the part families worry about most, so it is the part with the firmest rule: the crew never decides what a keepsake is. Photos, letters, paperwork, jewelry, cash, and anything that looks personal gets set aside in a designated keepsake spot as we find it, no matter where it turns up. You review that spot before anything leaves the property. In estate after estate, the shoebox of photos in the attic matters more than everything else in the house, and we treat it that way.

What is included in every estate cleanout

  • Free walkthrough with a firm quote and timeline before work starts
  • Room-by-room clearing with a designated keepsake set-aside
  • Donation routing for furniture, clothing, and kitchenware, including the Dallas Area Habitat ReStore, with receipts for the estate's records
  • Recycling for metal, electronics, and paper
  • Heavy single pieces handled under furniture removal and old units under appliance removal when that is all that is left

One thing we cannot load: the paint, solvents, motor oil, and pesticides that turn up in almost every garage. Those go to a city household chemical collection program, free for residents, and the TCEQ household hazardous waste guide for Texans will point you to the nearest one.

On a closing deadline? Tell us the date on the first call. We schedule estate cleanouts to finish before photography, inspection, or closing, and realtors across DFW use us for exactly that. Request a walkthrough and we will work backward from your date.

Before you text.

Common questions before you send the photo.

How long does an estate cleanout take?

Typically one to two days for a three-bedroom home. The walkthrough sets a firm timeline before anything starts.

What happens to photos, documents, and valuables you find?

They go to a designated keepsake spot for the family to review. The crew sets aside anything personal-looking; you make every keep-or-go decision.

Can estate items be donated, and do we get a receipt?

Yes. Usable items go to local donation partners and we provide receipts on request, which many families keep with the estate's records. Old computers, TVs, and phones get routed the way the EPA recommends rather than buried.

How is an estate cleanout quoted in DFW?

We walk the house with you first, look at what is actually going, and quote it firm before any work starts. Nothing is touched until you approve the number.
