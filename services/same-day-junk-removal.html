Call before noon, gone by dinner

Hero’s is based right in the northern suburbs, which is what makes real same-day pickup possible instead of a marketing line. Call before noon and you usually get that same afternoon across North Texas: Plano, Allen, Frisco, McKinney, Richardson, and the cities between. Call after noon and worst case is usually the next morning, first slot.

The deadlines we see every week

  • Closings and move-outs: the house must be empty by tomorrow's walkthrough. It will be.
  • Showings and photos: the listing goes live and the garage cannot be in the pictures.
  • Tenant turnovers: what the last tenant left behind is not your new tenant's problem.
  • Delivery collisions: the new couch arrives at 4pm and the old one has not left the living room.

Same-day, same price

No rush fee, no surcharge for the calendar working against you. Same-day jobs are quoted exactly like every other job: the crew looks at what you have and gives you a firm number, approved before we lift anything. The full walkthrough is in our guide to how junk removal works. Most rush calls turn out to be one heavy piece under furniture removal, a dead unit under appliance removal, or a whole house under estate cleanouts.

If the deadline is soft, check the free option first. Dallas collects brush and bulky items on a monthly schedule and Plano runs its own bulky waste collection. When the date on your calendar comes first, that is what we are for.

Want the best odds? Call in the morning, describe the pile or text a photo, and be reachable by phone. Those three things get more junk buried by sunset than anything else we know.

Before you text.

Common questions before you send the photo.

Is there a rush fee for same-day pickup?

No. Same-day is quoted exactly like every other job, approved before we load. The one thing speed cannot change is household hazardous waste, which goes to a city collection program instead of on the trailer. The TCEQ guide for Texas households explains why.

What is the cutoff for same-day?

Call before noon and same-day usually works across most of DFW. After noon, worst case is typically the next morning's first slot.

Do you do same-day on weekends?

Yes, 7 days a week, 7am to 7pm. Weekend slots go fastest, so call early.

What areas qualify?

The northern DFW suburbs: Plano, Allen, Frisco, McKinney, Richardson, Little Elm, Prosper, and the cities between.
