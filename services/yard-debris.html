Green waste and yard piles, hauled without the dumpster

Hero’s crews clear every kind of yard debris across DFW: tree branches and limbs, brush and hedge trimmings, leaves and grass, old fence panels and posts, and storm debris. We load it from wherever it sits, the backyard, the side yard, or the pile by the curb, and haul it straight off.

How pricing works (no surprises)

  • Standard yard debris: quoted firm on sight like everything else, before loading starts.
  • No weight surprises: there are no weight tickets and no hourly clock. If a load turns out heavier than it looked, that is on us, not you.
  • Full walkthrough: see how junk removal works in DFW.
  • Mixed loads: torn-out fence and deck boards ride along fine, and a full teardown falls under construction debris or shed and playset removal.

Junk crew vs renting a dumpster

There is a third option worth knowing about. Dallas picks up brush and bulky items on a monthly schedule and Plano runs bulky waste collection, both free for residents, and leaves and grass are better composted at home anyway, which the EPA has a straightforward guide to. For a one-time yard cleanup that cannot wait for the city calendar, a crew usually wins: no rental period, no street permit, no loading it yourself, and no metal box killing the lawn for a week. If the pile already exists, book the crew and it is gone in an afternoon.

What is included in every yard pickup

  • Two-person crew that does all the loading, from anywhere on the property
  • Loose piles loaded as they sit, no bagging required
  • A firm number with no weight surprises, quoted before we load

Seasonal cleanups: we clear the whole yard after a storm, a big trim, or an end-of-season cleanup in one visit. Storm work often turns up soaked furniture and a ruined fridge too, which come out under the same broad list. Mention it when you request a quote.

Before you text.

Common questions before you send the photo.

Do you take branches, brush, and yard waste?

Yes. Branches, brush piles, leaves, grass, and hedge trimmings are all standard loads, loaded loose as they sit, and you get a firm number before we start.

Do I need to bag or bundle it first?

No. We load loose piles as they sit. Bagging or stacking speeds loading, but pointing works.

Can you haul debris from the backyard?

Yes. The crew carries or wheelbarrows it from anywhere on the property as part of the quoted price.

Do you take old fencing and storm debris?

Yes. Downed fence panels and posts, fallen limbs, and storm debris are all standard yard loads. Send a photo and we quote it upfront.
