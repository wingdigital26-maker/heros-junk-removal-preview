Wylie, on the east side of the area

Wylie sits beside Lake Lavon on the east side of Collin County, past Plano, Allen and McKinney, and it is the eastern edge of where we go. Call before noon and the same afternoon is typical, and typical is the honest word rather than guaranteed, since access and job size are real variables. We take furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, hot tubs, sheds, yard debris, whole garages and whole estates. You point, we carry, we sweep.

What a Wylie job looks like

Wylie has both an older core and a lot of newer neighborhoods, so the calls run the full range: an original-owner garage that has been filling for years, a new-build move-in where the builder leftovers and the boxes never left, a backyard shed or hot tub that has outlived its use, and the single couch, mattress or fridge that the city will not take on its schedule. Each of those has its own page on this site, and each is quoted the same way, by photo, before anything is lifted.

  • Garages that have absorbed years. A long-owned house produces a garage nobody has fully emptied since they moved in. That is its own service for a reason, and it is covered under garage cleanouts.
  • Estates and downsizings. Families handling a parent's house. That work is quiet, unhurried, and handled under estate cleanouts. Humor stays out of it.
  • Furniture and appliances. Couches, dressers, beds, mattresses, fridges, washers and dryers, carried out and donated where they can be. See furniture removal and appliance removal.
  • Backyard teardowns. Hot tubs, sheds, playsets and decks that have outlived their use. Hot tub removal covers how those actually come out, and yard debris covers the brush and limb piles.

Driveways, gates and how we get to the load

Tell us what the approach looks like when you write: a driveway with room for a trailer, a side gate, a back fence that opens, or stairs inside the house. That one line removes most of the surprises on the day. If a piece has to come down stairs or through a narrow door, mention that too; sectionals, sleeper sofas and bed frames come apart and go out in pieces.

Use the city first when the city will do it free

Most cities in the area run some form of bulk or brush collection for residents on a schedule, and Wylie is no exception. If your load fits inside the city's program, use it. We would rather tell you the free option exists than take money you did not have to spend. Read the city's accepted list before you stack anything out front, because a pile the city declines just sits there.

Where the city stops working is volume and timing. Bulk pickup runs on a schedule and has limits, so a whole garage, a full estate, or anything with a closing date attached is where we come in. Paint, stain, solvents, motor oil, gasoline, pool chemicals, pesticides and propane tanks never go on our trailer; those go to a household chemical collection program. The statewide rules behind all of it sit with the TCEQ.

How you get your number

Send photos or we come look. You get a firm number before anything is lifted, disposal is inside that number, and the number holds once the work starts. No weight tickets, no hourly clock. The quote is the bill. When you are ready, tell us what you have.

Our own people do the carrying, not gig labor hired off an app that morning. Nash owns the company and works every job himself, which is why we are comfortable sending a crew inside a house rather than only working a driveway.

Usable furniture and working appliances go to local donation partners. Metal and cardboard go to recyclers. More than half of a typical load stays out of the landfill, and where your junk actually goes walks through what happens to it after the trailer pulls away.

Just one item? Two of the most common single-item calls in Wylie get their own quick page: appliance pickup in Wylie and yard waste removal in Wylie. Anything else is quoted the same way, by photo, and furniture removal and appliance removal cover the rest.

Selling a Wylie house? Clear it before the listing photos rather than before the first showing. The pictures go up first and they stay up. What to clear before the listing photos covers the room-by-room version.

Common questions before you send the photo.

Do you serve all of Wylie?

Yes, from the older streets near the original town center to the newer neighborhoods further out. Pickups run seven days a week.

How fast can you get to a Wylie address?

Wylie is the eastern edge of our area, so calling before noon usually means the same afternoon, with an honest window when the schedule is full. Typical rather than guaranteed, because access and job size are real.

What if the city will take it for free?

Then use the city. Wylie runs its own residential collection programs on a schedule, and if your pile fits inside them we will say so. Where the city stops is volume and timing, and that is where we come in.

Do you take paint and old chemicals from a Wylie garage?

No, and no licensed hauler can. Paint, stain, solvents, motor oil, gasoline, pool chemicals, pesticides and propane tanks go to a household chemical collection program, not on our trailer.

Can you clear an older Wylie garage or shed?

Yes. A garage that has been filling for years is its own service for a reason, and it is covered under garage cleanouts.

Do you do same-day work in Wylie?
