Questions, answered.

Anything else, call (214) 277-9069. Todd or Nash picks up.

How do I get a price?
Text a photo of what needs to go to (214) 277-9069 with your city. You get a price back by text. No form, no visit first.
What if I do not have a photo?
Call (214) 277-9069 and describe it. A rough list of what is there is enough to talk it through.
Can it go today?
Often, when the schedule allows. We run 7am to 8pm every day, so text early and ask for the soonest slot.
What do you take?
Furniture, appliances, mattresses, couches, hot tubs, estate and garage cleanouts, construction debris and yard debris. If you are not sure, send the photo anyway.
Where do you go?
Frisco, Plano, McKinney, Prosper, Allen and Little Elm. Nearby addresses are worth a text.
Where does it all end up?
The landfill is the last stop, not the first. Usable furniture and appliances go to local donation partners, and metal, cardboard and electronics go to recyclers.
Is there anything you cannot take?
Hazardous materials cannot legally ride on the trailer. If you are not sure whether something can go, send the photo and ask.
